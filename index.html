ASKED TOO LATE

Questions people ask after it’s too late.

Can a PDF lie?+

Fluently. It can also blush: metadata, fonts, layer history and math all leave fingerprints. We read those instead of the prose.

Is a signature proof?+

It is proof that someone can draw. Proof of identity lives in the registry, the thread, the timeline and the hash. We check those.

What if there’s no evidence?+

Then we say so, loudly. “Unknown” is a verdict. “Probably fine” is a hope.

Do you detect AI?+

We detect wrong. AI is just one of the newer ways to be wrong. A human with a copy of Word has been doing it for decades.

Will it slow us down?+

A fake invoice costs weeks. Reading the evidence costs minutes. Choose your delay.
