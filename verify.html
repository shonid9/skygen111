    THE FOUR VERDICTS

    We only speak four words.

    Every finding lands in exactly one of these. There is no “probably”.

    Confirmed

    The source agrees. Not the sender, not the tone, not the logo. A record that doesn’t care about the outcome.

    Conflict

    Two things can’t both be true. The registry says one director, the contract says another. We show both and pick neither.

    Suspicious

    Nothing is proven wrong. Everything is worth a second look. A reused signature, a three-day-old domain, a tax rate that doesn’t exist.

    Unknown

    Unknown is a verdict. “Probably fine” is not. If there’s no evidence yet, we say so, and we say what would settle it.
