WHAT GETS CHECKED

Four layers of legal review.

Each layer answers a different question. Confusing them is how forgeries get admitted.

INTEGRITY

File & version history

Document properties, edits, page changes, structural anomalies, embedded objects and version differences.

ASKSIs this the file it claims to be?
CONTENT

Internal consistency

Names, dates, amounts, clauses, defined terms, references, contradictions and missing supporting material.

ASKSDoes the document agree with itself?
FACTS

External verification

Cited entities, addresses, company details, public records, court references and other available trusted sources.
