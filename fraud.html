HOW A STORY FAILS

Four ways the chain breaks.

We don’t need the whole story to be false. One link is enough. Scroll: the cards stack like evidence on a desk.

01

The timeline folds

The invoice is dated before the order. The email arrives before the meeting it describes. Time is the one witness that never changes its statement.

02

The identity forks

The name matches. The domain is three days old. The registry lists a different director. One person, two versions, zero overlap.

03

The money moves first

Payment requested before delivery, before contract, before anyone could ask a question. Speed is the tell.

04

The evidence repeats

The same signature pixel-for-pixel on three exhibits. The same testimonial on four websites. Copies are confessions.

A good story explains everything. Evidence explains what happened.
