FRAUD & DECEPTION

Break the story.
Test every part.

Fraud often works because one believable item supports another. EMET ONE should split the story into separate claims, verify each one, and show where the chain breaks.

The system is not a lie detector. It is an evidence engine: claims, files, identities, timelines, amounts, and sources are checked separately and then compared.
01

Extract claims

Who, what, when, where, how much, and what supposedly happened.

02

Find evidence

Files, messages, records, registries, public sources, and connected systems.

03

Compare

Look for impossible dates, identity conflicts, duplicate evidence, altered values, or unsupported assertions.

04

Score confidence

Confirmed, likely, suspicious, conflicting, or still unknown, with reasons attached.

IMPERSONATION

Fake identity

Check whether names, domains, contact information, and organizational claims line up with trusted sources.

ALTERED EVIDENCE

Edited proof

Look for changed numbers, inconsistent formatting, image manipulation signals, and source mismatches.

CONTRADICTIONS

Story conflicts

Compare messages, documents, dates, locations, and payment records to find where the narrative stops matching the evidence.
