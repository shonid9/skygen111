04

Return an evidence map

Show confirmed facts, conflicts, suspicious signals, source quality, and unresolved gaps.

Confidence is not certainty

A trustworthy system should say when evidence is weak. “Not verified” is often more useful than pretending something is true or false.

Human review stays visible

High stakes legal, financial, identity, or fraud decisions should preserve source links, rationale, and a clear path for human review.
